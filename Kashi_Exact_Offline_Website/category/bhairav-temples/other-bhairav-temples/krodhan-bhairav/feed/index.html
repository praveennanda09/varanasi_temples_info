Some people say that Krodhan Bhairav is the small Bhairav located adjacent to Kamakhya Devi in the temple nearby.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open from 05.00 a.m. to 12.00 Noon and from 04.00 p.m. to 12.00 p.m.  Aarties are conducted in the morning and evening.

 

POOJARIES OF THE TEMPLE

Mahant Vijay Puri and Acharya Shambu Saran are available near the temple premises and can be contacted at Mobile No. (0)9839152539 from 09.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon and from 06.00 p.m. to 09.00 p.m.  According to the Mahantji, devotees contact the temple authorities for Rahu Shanti, relief from Kaal Sarp Dosh, Tantra Nivaran etc., for which Japam (repetition of a set of mantras) is being undertaken at the temple.
