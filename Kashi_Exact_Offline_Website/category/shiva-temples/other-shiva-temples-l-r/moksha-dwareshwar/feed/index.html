Moksha Dwareshwar – Varanasi Temples ../../../../../index.html Temples of Varanasi as referred to in Kashi Khand Thu, 19 Jun 2025 05:16:46 +0000 en-US hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=4.6.29 MOKSHA DWARESHWAR ../../../../../shiva-temples/moksha-dwareshwar/index.html Mon, 10 May 2010 15:34:05 +0000 ../../../../../p=428.html According to Kashi Khand, Chapter 94, a devotee who worships Moksha Dwareshwar Ling will always find Moksha.

By Moksha we may mean release from the cycle of rebirth in the world.  Great importance is attached by old timers to such practices that avoid rebirth, and death in Kashi is supposed to grant a person release from this ordeal.

LOCATION OF MOKSHA DWARESHWAR

Moksha Dwareshwar is located at Ck.34/10, Lahouri Tola, near Phoota Ganesh.

This address is as of 2017.  Presently this ling could have been displaced due to expansion of Kashi Vishwanath Corridor.

TYPES OF POOJA

The Ling is located inside a house and people can have darshan.  No specific details of Pooja etc. were given by the local people as it was inside a house.
