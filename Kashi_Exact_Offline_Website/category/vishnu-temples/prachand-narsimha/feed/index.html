Prachand means huge/big.  Devotees worshipping Prachand Narsimha are cleansed of all their sins irrespective of their magnitude.

(PRACHAND NARSIMHA)

 

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 61, Shlok 190

 

प्रचण्ड नरसिम्होऽहं चण्डभैरवपूर्वत :

प्रचण्डमप्यघं  कृत्वा निष्पाप्मा स्यात्तदर्चनात्

 

Lord Vishnu says, “Towards the east of Chand Bhairav, I am available as Prachand Narsimha and by worshipping this deity, the devotee will become sinless even after committing gravest of sins”. 

 

———————————————————————————————————

 

LOCATION OF PRACHAND NARSIMHA

Prachand Narsimha is located at B.1/151, Jagannath Temple, Assi Ghat. People can travel upto this place in a rickshaw.

TYPES OF POOJA

The temple is open from 06.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon and from 03.00 p.m. to 09.00 p.m.  Aarties are held in the morning and evening.
