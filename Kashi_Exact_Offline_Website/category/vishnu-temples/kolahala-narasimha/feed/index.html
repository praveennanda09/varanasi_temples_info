(KOLAHAL NARASIMHA)

By worshipping this Lord, the sins will get topsy turvy and finally vanish.

LOCATION OF KOLAHALA NARSIMHA

Kolahala Narsimha is located at Ck.8/181 above Siddhi Vinayak, inside Narsimha Mutt.  People can approach this place by traveling upto Chowk in a rickshaw and then on foot.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open from 04.00 a.m. to 11.00 a.m. and from 05.00 p.m. to 09.30 p.m.  Aarties are held in the morning and evening.
