By worshipping this Lord, the devotees will attain ultimate knowledge in all spheres of activities.

LOCATION OF GYAN MADHAV

Gyan Madhav is located at Ck.35/21, Vishwanath Temple compound near Akshay Vat. The narrow lane or Gully approaching the temple is known as Vishwanath Gully.  For directions to reach this place, kindly refer Visweshwar Temple.

TYPES OF POOJA

Devotees can worship and pray this Lord as per their own convenience.
