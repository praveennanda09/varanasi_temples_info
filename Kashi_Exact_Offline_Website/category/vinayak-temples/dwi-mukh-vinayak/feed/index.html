(DWI MUKH VINAYAK)

Dwi Mukh Vinayak is located at D-51/90, West of Saamba Aditya, Suraj Kund. Worshipping this lord will give all round prosperity.  It is almost in the temple premises. Devotees can travel upto Suraj Kund by rickshaw.

———————————————————————————————————–

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 57, Shlok 101

 

क्षिप्रप्रसादनादैन्द्यां  द्वितुण्डो गणनायक:

तस्य संदर्शनात्पुंसां भवेच्छ्री: सर्वतोमुखी

 

Dwimukh Vinayak is to the east of Kshipra Prasadan Ganesha, by worshipping him, people get all kinds of prosperity.
