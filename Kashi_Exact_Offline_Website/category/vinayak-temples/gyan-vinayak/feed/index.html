(GYAN VINAYAK)

Gyan Vinayak is located at Khowa Gulli Chowraha at Ck.28/4 Langlishwar temple.  Gyan Vinayak guards Kashi against evil forces, according to Kashi Khand.   Devotees can travel upto Chowk by rickshaw and walk on foot to this place via Khatri Medical Hall.

TYPES OF POOJA

The temple is open from 08.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m. and from 06.00 p.m. to 08.00 p.m.  Aarties are held in the morning and evening.
