RAJ PUTRA VINAYAK

Raj Putra Vinayak is located at A-37/48, on the Road, Raj Ghat Fort. This will come near Adi Keshav Temple.  Devotees can travel upto this place by auto rickshaw, car or cycle rickshaw.

 

By worshipping Raj Putra Vinayak, even a vanquished king can regain his lost kingdom.  In the present day scenario this may apply to devotees who may be aspiring to get high positions.

 (RAJ PUTRA VINAYAK)

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 57, Shlok 76

 

खर्वाख्यान्नैर्ऋते  भागे राजपुत्रो विनायक:

भ्रष्टराज्यं च राजानं राजानं कुरुतेऽर्चित:

 

By worshiping Rajputra Ganesha on the southwest corner of Kharva Vinayak, the deposed king also gets the kingdom again.
