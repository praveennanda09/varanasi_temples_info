## Shared scriptural background

The site attributes the protector Bhairav group to the narrative after the Goddess defeated Durgasura. According to the overview, the Goddess created nine Shaktis to protect Kashi, and eight Bhairav forms were stationed at different places in the city. The site says these forms protect devotees from evil and are associated with courage, mental strength, happiness and prosperity. The overview cites Kashi Khand Chapter 72.
