## Dashamaha Vidya checklist

The commonly listed ten Mahavidyas are Kali, Tara, Tripurasundari/Shodashi, Bhuvaneshwari, Bhairavi, Chhinnamasta, Dhumavati, Bagalamukhi, Matangi and Kamala. The site's menu explicitly includes **Tripura Bhairavi** and **Varahi Devi**, but the latter is not one of the standard ten Mahavidyas. The available evidence in this pass does not validate a one-to-one list of ten Kashi temples for the ten Mahavidyas. Do not treat the list above as verified temple correspondences. Verify each deity's local shrine name, address, article and route before pilgrimage.
