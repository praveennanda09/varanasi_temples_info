(MODA VINAYAK)

Moda Vinayak is located at  Ck.31/12, Kashi Karvat Temple, Naipali Khapra. Devotees can travel upto Chowk by rickshaw and walk on foot to this place.

Moda Vinayak removes all obstacles in life.
