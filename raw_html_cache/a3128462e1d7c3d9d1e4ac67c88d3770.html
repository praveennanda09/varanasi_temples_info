Dehli Vinayak

Dehli Vinayak is located at a village which is almost more than 20 kms. away from the heart of Varanasi city.  Dehli in hindi means a threshold.  Worshipping Dehli Vinayak will remove all obstacles coming in the way of the devotees.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.
