Raj Putra Vinayak – Varanasi Temples https://varanasitemples.in Temples of Varanasi as referred to in Kashi Khand Thu, 19 Jun 2025 05:16:46 +0000 en-US hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=4.6.29 RAJ PUTRA VINAYAK https://varanasitemples.in/vinayak-temples/raj-putra-vinayak/ Fri, 07 May 2010 16:41:43 +0000 http://varanasi-temples.com/?p=107 Raj Putra Vinayak is located at A-37/48, on the Road, Raj Ghat Fort. This will come near Adi Keshav Temple.  Devotees can travel upto this place by auto rickshaw, car or cycle rickshaw.

 

By worshipping Raj Putra Vinayak, even a vanquished king can regain his lost kingdom.  In the present day scenario this may apply to devotees who may be aspiring to get high positions.

 (RAJ PUTRA VINAYAK)

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 57, Shlok 76

 

खर्वाख्यान्नैर्ऋते  भागे राजपुत्रो विनायक:

भ्रष्टराज्यं च राजानं राजानं कुरुतेऽर्चित:

 

By worshiping Rajputra Ganesha on the southwest corner of Kharva Vinayak, the deposed king also gets the kingdom again.

The place of worship is open in the morning from 06.00 a.m. to 07.00 a.m. and 07.00 p.m. to 08.00 p.m. Aarties are performed in the morning and evening.  However, devotees  can worship this deity throughout the day through grill door.
