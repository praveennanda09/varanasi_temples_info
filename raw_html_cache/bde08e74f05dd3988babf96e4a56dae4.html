(TRI MUKH VINAYAK)

Tri Mukh Vinayak is located at D-59/94, Sigra at Tripurantakeshwar. Tri Mukh Vinayak is capable of removing all fears.   As per Kashi Khand, one face is of a monkey, second of a Lion and third face is of an elephant. For directions to reach this place, kindly refer to Tripurantakeshwar.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves. The idol shows three trunks of the Elephant.
