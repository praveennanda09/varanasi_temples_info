Atyugra Narsimha – Varanasi Temples https://varanasitemples.in Temples of Varanasi as referred to in Kashi Khand Thu, 19 Jun 2025 05:16:46 +0000 en-US hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=4.6.29 ATYUGRA NARSIMHA https://varanasitemples.in/vishnu-temples/atyugra-narsimha/ Tue, 11 May 2010 10:47:51 +0000 http://varanasi-temples.com/?p=639 Towards the north of Kamleshwar Siva, Lord Vishnu is present in the form of Atyugra Narsimha.

Devotees worshipping this Lord will be cleansed of all their sins. By splitting Atyugra readers can see the joining of two words Ati and Ugra. This shows the extreme power of this Lord.

 

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 61, Shlok 193

 

अत्युग्र नरसिंहोऽहं  कलशेश्वरपश्चिमे

अत्युग्रमपि पापौघं हरामि श्रद्धयार्चित:

 

(Lord Vishnu says,) To the west of Kalasheshwar I am called Atyugra Narasimha. If I am worshipped with devotion, I destroy even the group of Atyugra (terrible) sins.

 

———————————————————————————————————

 

LOCATION OF ATYUGRA NARSIMHA

Atyugra Narsimha is located at Ck.8/21, Gomath. People can approach this place by traveling upto Chowk in a rickshaw and then on foot. Gomath is a famous locality.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open from 06.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m. and from 04.00 p.m. to  10.00 p.m. Devotees can pray and perform pooja by their own.
