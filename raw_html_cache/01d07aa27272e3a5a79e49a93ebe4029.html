Asha Vinayak – Varanasi Temples https://varanasitemples.in Temples of Varanasi as referred to in Kashi Khand Thu, 19 Jun 2025 05:16:46 +0000 en-US hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=4.6.29 ASHA VINAYAK https://varanasitemples.in/vinayak-temples/asha-vinayak-3/ Sat, 08 May 2010 03:17:31 +0000 http://varanasi-temples.com/?p=192 Asha Vinayak is located at D-3/71, Mir Ghat, Bare Hanuman Temple.  This falls in the vicinity of Vishalakshi Devi Temple. All desires of the devotees will be fulfilled if they offer devout prayers to Asha Vinayak. Devotees can travel upto Dasaswamedh Vishwanath Gully by rickshaw and walk on foot to this place through Vishalakshi Gauri Temple, which is a famous landmark. Alternatively they can travel by boat upto Mir Ghat and walk up the steps and reach the place.

(ASHA VINAYAK)
