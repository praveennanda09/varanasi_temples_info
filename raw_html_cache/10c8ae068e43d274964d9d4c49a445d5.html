(DURMUKH VINAYAK)

Durmukh Vinayak was earlier located at Ck.34/60,  near Kashi Karvat Temlple. Now it is relocated and placed in extended Kashi Vishwanath Corridor.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.
