(GANGA ADITYA)

It is believed that even today Lord Surya is residing at the above place reciting Stotras of Ganges and blessing his devotees.  The form of Surya at the Ghat of Ganges came to be known as Ganga Aditya.

A staunch devotee, who worships Ganga Aditya will never face any mishap and will never be cheated by anyone. (Kashi Khand, Chapter 51).

 

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 51, Shlok 104

 

गङ्गादित्यं समाराध्य वाराणस्यां नरोत्तम:

न जातु दुर्गतिं क्वापि लभते न च रोगभाक्

 

The good man (sincere devotee) who worships Gangaditya in Varanasi neither has to suffer any misfortune nor does he suffer from any disease.

 

———————————————————————————————————

 

LOCATION OF GANGA ADITYA

Ganga Aditya is located at No. 1/68, below Nepali Mandir, Lalita Ghat.  The deity is situated in a room covered with iron grill and facing the river Ganges. From Vishwanath temple, a devotee should walk to the Ghat steps and it is advisable to ask for Nepali Pasupatinath temple for location. Nepali Pasupatinath temple is a landmark below which, devotees can find Ganga Aditya.
