(VARAD VINAYAK)

Varad Vinayak is located at A-13/19, near Naya Mahadev Kali Mandir, Prahlad Ghat. Varad Vinayak is capable of blessing devotees with divine wish.  Devotees may travel upto Prahlad Ghat Chauraha by rickshaw and then on foot.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.
