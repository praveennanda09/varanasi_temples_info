(SUMUKH VINAYAK)

Sumukh Vinayak is presently located inside the extended Kashi Vishwanath Corridor.  Worshipping this Vinayak will give all sorts of happiness to devotees.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.
