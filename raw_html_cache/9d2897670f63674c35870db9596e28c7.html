(LAKSHMI NARSIMHA)

Such devotees would be blessed with all round happiness, prosperity and release from rebirth. The Teerth is not in existence as on date.

LOCATION OF LAKSHMI NARSIMHA

Lakshmi Narsimha is located at K.20/159, Raj Mandir, inside Dakshin Mukhi (one eyed) Hanumanji temple. This temple is near Kashi Vyayamshala / Suvodhini Vidya Mandir. Raj Mandir is a locality near Gai Ghat.

TYPES OF POOJA

The temple is open for worship from 06.00 a.m. to 12.00 Noon and from 02.00 p.m. to 10.00 p.m.  Aarties are performed in the morning and evening.
