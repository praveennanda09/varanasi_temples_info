(PAAS PANI VINAYAK)

 

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 57, Shlok 64

 

काश्या: सदोत्तराशायां पाश्पाणिर्विनायक:

विनायकान् पाषयन्ति भक्त्या काशीनिवासिनाम्

 

Paashpani Vinayak is situated in the northern direction of Kashi. He removes all the troubles of the people of Kashi by just worshipping him.

 

———————————————————————————————————

 

In the Cantonment area, if a devotee proceeds on the road where various posh hotels are located, he will come across Radisson, Ideal Towers, Hotel Clarks etc. and JHV Mall.  If he proceeds further on the road (known as Cariappa Marg), he will come across a turning to the right where there is an indicator reading “Bheeshma Chandi Devi temple”. Without taking the right turn, if he proceeds further he will come to Paas Pani Vinayak on the right side of the road. There is a stone slab on the road indicating this temple.
