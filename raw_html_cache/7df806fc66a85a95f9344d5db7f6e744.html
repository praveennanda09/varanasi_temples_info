Kooshmanda Vinayak

Kooshmanda Vinayak is located at Phoolwaria village which once considered a village,  is now practically part of the city.  Worshipping Kooshmanda Vinayak will bring happiness to the devotees.

TYPES OF POOJA

 The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.
