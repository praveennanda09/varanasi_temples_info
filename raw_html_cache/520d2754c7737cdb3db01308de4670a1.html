By worshipping Bheeshma Keshav even the most dangerous obstacles of the devotee will be removed.

 

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 61, Shlok 24

 

भीष्मकेशवनामाऽहं वृद्धकालेशपश्चिमे

उपसर्गान हरे भीषमान् सेवितो भक्तियुक्तित:

 

Lord Vishnu says, “By stationing myself as Bheeshma Keshav towards the west of Vriddh Kaaleshwar, I remove the dangerous disturbances of devotees who worship me with devotion.” 

 

———————————————————————————————————

 

LOCATION OF BHEESHMA KESHAV

Bheeshma Keshav is located in the temple premises of Mrityunjay Mahadev at K.52/39, near Vriddha Kaleshwar.  This place of worship is open throughout.
