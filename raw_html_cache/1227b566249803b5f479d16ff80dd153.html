Durg Vinayak is a stand alone temple with two entrances and a lot of devotees regularly come for offering prayers. Devotees can travel upto this place by cycle rickshaw or auto rickshaw and famous landmark is Durga Kund which one can never miss.

TYPES OF POOJA

The temple is open from 05.00 a.m. to 12.00 Noon and 04.00 p.m. to 10.00 p.m.  Mangala Aarti is held at 05.00 a.m., Bhog at 12.00 Noon, Sandhya Aarti at 07.00 p.m. and Shayan Aarti at 10.00 p.m.  The aarties are widely attended by many persons.  Staunch devotees believe that worshipping Durg Vinayak during or before any job/project, will certainly give success in that venture.
