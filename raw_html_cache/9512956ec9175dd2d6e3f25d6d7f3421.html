Pitar Kunda is a famous locality. Worshipping this Vinayak will bring all round success to the devotees.

(KSHIPRA PRASAD VINAYAK)

——————————————————————————————————-

 

Kashi Khand, Chapter 57, Shlok 92

 

प्राच्यां पञ्चास्यत: पायात् पुरीं क्षिप्रप्रसादन:

क्षिप्रप्रसादनार्चात: क्षिप्रं सिद्ध्यन्ति सिद्धय:

 

Kshipra Prasadan Ganesha, to the east of Panchmukh Ganesha, protects this city. By worshipping him, all the achievements are attained very quickly.

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open practically throughout and devotees can perform pooja by themselves.  Shri Ganesh Prasad is the Poojari of the temple.
