Koot Dant Vinayak – Varanasi Temples https://varanasitemples.in Temples of Varanasi as referred to in Kashi Khand Thu, 19 Jun 2025 05:16:46 +0000 en-US hourly 1 https://wordpress.org/?v=4.6.29 KOOT DANT VINAYAK https://varanasitemples.in/vinayak-temples/koot-dant-vinayak/ Sat, 08 May 2010 02:37:57 +0000 http://varanasi-temples.com/?p=144 Koot Dant Vinayak is located at No. B-3/335, Kina Ram Ashram, Krim Kund, Shivala, Varanasi.  Devotees can travel upto this place by autorickshaw or cycle rickshaw. This Vinayak removes even the toughest obstacles and guards Kashi area.

TYPES OF POOJA

The Ashram where the Koot Dant Vinayak is located, is open from 05.00 a.m. to 12.00 Noon and 04.00 p.m. to 10.00 p.m.  The timings may vary during summer and winter.  The devotees can perform pooja by themselves as there is no specific priest.
