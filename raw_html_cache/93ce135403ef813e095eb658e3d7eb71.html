At the original place of this temple, a shopping complex, known as TSM Complex,  has been constructed and a small temple has been constructed in the premises on the second floor.  Worshipping Heramba Vinayak will fulfill the desires of devotees.

(HERAMB VINAYAK)

TYPES OF POOJA

The place of worship is open from 08.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon and from 06.00 p.m. to 08.00 p.m.  Aarties are held in the morning and evening.
